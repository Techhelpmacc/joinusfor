How it works

Send one link. That's the whole job.

We build you a site with your names on it — your story, your timings, your details. You choose how it looks from four designs.

Your guests find their invitation, say whether they're coming, pick a meal and tell you about the nut allergy. You watch the numbers add up without chasing a soul.

On the day there's a QR code on the tables. Guests send you the photos they took, straight from their phones. You decide which ones appear.

Afterwards the same link quietly becomes your album — the photographer's pictures and everyone else's, in one place, for as long as you want it.
